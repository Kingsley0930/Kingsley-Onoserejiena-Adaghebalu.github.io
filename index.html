Adaghebalu Kingsley Onoserejiena

Historian • Teacher • Writer • Researcher

Biography

Adaghebalu Kingsley Onoserejiena is a Nigerian historian, teacher, writer and researcher from Edo State, Nigeria. He was born on 22 March 1996 in Uromi, Edo State.

He has developed interests in history, education, culture, research, writing and the documentation of the history and heritage of his people.

Early Life and Background

Adaghebalu Kingsley Onoserejiena is a native of Ebagha, Ubierumu-Oke, in Esan North-East Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria.

Education

  • First School Leaving Certificate — 2009
  • West African Senior School Certificate — 2016
  • Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in History and International Studies, Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma — 2022
  • Master of Arts (M.A.) in History, University of Benin — in view

Career and Professional Interests

His professional interests include teaching, historical research, writing, communication, cultural studies and community history.

Research Interests

  • Esan history and culture
  • Uromi history
  • Traditional institutions and monarchy
  • Nigerian history
  • Colonial and post-colonial history
  • African history and heritage

Military and National Service

Adaghebalu Kingsley Onoserejiena completed the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) programme on 27 August 2025.

Writing and Public Contributions

He has written and contributed to discussions on history, politics, education, culture, society and personal development. His work reflects an interest in documenting people, places, institutions and historical experiences.

Personal Profile

Adaghebalu Kingsley Onoserejiena is passionate about education, historical knowledge, writing and the preservation of cultural heritage.
